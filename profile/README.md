# Anvil Coder

**From ticket to reviewed pull request.** Anvil Coder takes a ticket from your tracker, plans the work,
writes the change in small steps and opens a draft pull request, with your team's rules applied at every step.
Your reviewers read it commit by commit and decide what goes in.

- **Starts from your backlog:** a ticket set to "ready" starts the run; no session to open or accompany.
- **Your rules, every step:** guardrails are part of every prompt, and machine-readable rules are checked
  against the result.
- **One commit per step, one report per run:** reviewers see what was done and why, and what each step cost.
- **Your code host, your model key:** GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket or Forgejo; the organisation's own key for the
  model provider.
- **Built and hosted in Switzerland** by [Halvic Labs](https://halvic.ch).

repositories are read-only mirrors; issues and questions go to the contact on the website.
